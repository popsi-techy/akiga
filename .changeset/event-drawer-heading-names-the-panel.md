The connection-event drawer's heading names the panel instead of repeating the card.

It read "Accounts Fetch" — the label on the card that opened the drawer, the first row of
the rail behind it, and the value sitting in the Event name field two inches below. The one
word on screen that was already said three times, and the only one that never said what the
reader was now looking at.

"Accounts Fetch calls" uses the rail's own noun: this panel is that event's calls, and the
tabs describe one of them.

The subtitle said "Inbound. Add the calls IGA makes, then describe each one." — direction
the reader already had (the card sits under an Inbound heading, and the label says *Fetch*)
and an instruction for a control that is now at the top of the rail. It says the two things
nothing else on screen does: which system is at the other end, and that reading the answer
is part of the job. "The requests IGA sends to Google Workspace, and how it reads what comes
back."
