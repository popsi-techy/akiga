The attribute-mapping table loses its permanent syntax paragraph.

"A transformation is optional. Reference attributes in square brackets…" sat under the table
at all times — a sentence you read once and then scrolled past for the rest of the
connector's life, taking two lines of a drawer that is mostly form.

The sentence itself is worth keeping, because the expression syntax is documented nowhere
else, so it moved onto the Transformation column header as an info tooltip: on the thing it
describes, reachable by pointer or keyboard, and out of the way once you know it.
