Reports is four tabs again. Schedules is back on the hub as a table, not a nested page.

The activity band and section rail made the catalogue feel like a dashboard with a filing cabinet beside it, and they hid the one table that answers “what fires next / what skipped”. Operational, Compliance, Custom reports and Schedules are peers; the next-run line sits above the schedule rows it comes from. `/iga/reports/schedules` redirects to `?tab=schedules`.
