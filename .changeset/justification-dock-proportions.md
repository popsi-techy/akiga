The request wizard's justification dock gives the reason room to be written and the rest of the
column to the dropzone.

The justification box floored at three rows (60px) — enough to see one line of what you had typed
while writing the paragraph a reviewer is going to weigh. Five rows takes it to 100px. It is a floor,
not a height: the field still grows as the reason gets longer.

Below it, the attachment field takes whatever the column has left. Empty, that is a real dropzone —
236px of it in a 466px column — with the clip mark centred and the prompt beneath it, rather than a
54px row with a lot of nothing under it.
