Application onboarding uses Emergency Access V1's flow: tabs plus a right-hand setup checklist, not a left rail.

A draft application hid its tab strip behind `DetailRail`, so section switching and remaining work were the same sidebar. Tabs are the navigator again. The checklist docks on the right (Required to connect / Additional), toggled from the book control next to More. Overview, accounts and entitlements stay off a draft until Connect — they would be empty lists pretending to be work.
