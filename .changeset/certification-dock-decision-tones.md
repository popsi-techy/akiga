The bulk decision icons in Access Certification's selection dock now carry the same green and red as the per-row buttons — Belongs to me and Certify in success, Does not belong to me and Revoke in danger. They were grey, so the same decision looked like a different action depending on whether you took it on one row or on a selection. Only the glyph is tinted; the row buttons' bordered box would be a box inside the pill.
