The connection-event rail's head becomes a heading over one toolbar: search leading, Add
opposite it.

It was three rows deep — a CALLS heading with Add beside it, then the search field, then
the list — and 105px of chrome at the top of a 288px column before a single call appeared.
Add was up there for want of anywhere better to put it; opposite the search is where every
other list in the product keeps its add action.

The heading stays, in the shape the flow board's rail already uses: the name of the list at
`body-sm-strong`, and its count on the right in caption grey. A heading rather than the
overline it was, because it is the top of a column rather than a label inside one, and the
count is the one thing about this column nothing else on screen says. Head comes down to
87px, the difference going to the list.

`secondary` rather than the filled primary that pattern usually gets: the drawer already
spends its one primary on Save, and adding a call is not what you opened the drawer to do.
The field and the button share the 36px control height, so they sit on one line.
