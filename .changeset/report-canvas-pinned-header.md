The report canvas gets a docked toolbar and a scrolling grey body.

The actions toolbar (download, schedule, expand) is docked at the top of the report card, and below it the report sits on a grey canvas that scrolls — its sections read as cards on the canvas, and the report's own title, description and period ride at the top of the document where they belong.
