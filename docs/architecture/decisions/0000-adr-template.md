## Context

What is the problem or force that requires a decision? What constraints apply (technical,
product, UX, accessibility, timeline)? State facts, not opinions. Link related ADRs.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly and in the present tense: "We will …". Be specific enough that a
future AI or engineer can act on it without asking.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or newly required as a result? Include follow-up work, new rules
this creates, and anything the Design System or IGA product must now do or must not do.

## Alternatives considered

For each realistic alternative: a one-line description and why it was **not** chosen. Rejecting
options explicitly is as valuable as choosing one.
