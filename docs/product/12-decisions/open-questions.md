# Open Questions & Assumptions Log

A living record of what we **don't yet know** and what we're **assuming until told otherwise**.
The purpose is to keep assumptions visible so they don't silently harden into "truth."

> **Status:** structure defined, no entries yet.

## How to use this log
- When a decision is blocked on missing information, add an **Open Question**.
- When you proceed on an unconfirmed premise, add an **Assumption** (per the constitution:
  never make a *silent* assumption).
- When an item is resolved, record the resolution and, if it's a durable product decision,
  promote it to a **PDR**. Keep the resolved row for history.
