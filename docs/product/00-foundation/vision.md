# Product Vision

## Mission
Give enterprises continuous, provable control over **who has access to what — and why** —
through a governance platform that is fast for end users, trustworthy for auditors, and simple
for administrators.

## Long-term vision
The default control plane for workforce access: every grant is requested, justified, approved,
time-bound, reviewed, and auditable. Governance shifts from periodic spreadsheet exercises to a
continuous, low-friction background process the business barely notices — until an auditor asks,
and the answer is one click away.

## Product goals
1. **Reduce standing access** — make least-privilege the path of least resistance.
2. **Make approvals fast and informed** — approvers see risk, context, and recommendation in one
   place; decisions take seconds, not days.
3. **Make audits boring** — every action produces immutable evidence; certifications and reports
   export on demand.
4. **Automate the identity lifecycle** — joiners, movers, and leavers get the right access
   automatically, on time.
5. **Surface risk, not noise** — highlight the few things that matter (SoD conflicts, high-risk
   grants, orphan accounts, overdue reviews).

## Success metrics (product-level)
These are the outcomes the product optimizes for — mirrored as dashboard KPIs in `metrics.json`.

| Metric | Direction | Why it matters |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| Time-to-access (request → fulfilled) | ↓ | User productivity & satisfaction |
| Approval turnaround time | ↓ | Bottleneck / friction indicator |
| % access that is time-bound | ↑ | Standing-access reduction |
| Certification completion rate & on-time % | ↑ | Audit readiness |
| Open SoD violations & mean age | ↓ | Risk exposure |
| Orphan / dormant accounts | ↓ | Attack surface |
| Auto-provisioned vs. manual (JML) | ↑ | Lifecycle automation |

> This document is intentionally short. It is the north star everything else must serve; if a
> feature doesn't advance a goal above, question it.
