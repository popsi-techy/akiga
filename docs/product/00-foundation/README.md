# 00 · Foundation

The product's north star and its guardrails. Read this before anything else.

> **Populated.**

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| `vision.md` | **Mission, long-term vision, product goals, and success metrics.** The single statement of what the product is for and how we'll know it's working. Everything downstream must serve this. |
| `scope-and-assumptions.md` | **What is in scope, out of scope, and explicitly a non-goal.** Plus the assumptions and constraints (no backend, frontend-driven prototype, mock data). Prevents scope creep and tells the AI where the edges are. |
| `roadmap-and-phasing.md` | **Build order and prioritization (MoSCoW).** Which modules/capabilities come first and why. Tells any AI what to build next when unprompted. |

Feeds no registry directly, but constrains all of them.
